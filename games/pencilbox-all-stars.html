HOW TO PLAY

The goal

Hits raise your opponent's damage %. The higher it is, the further they fly. Knock them past the edge of the screen to take a stock. Last fighter with stocks wins.

Ledges

Fall near a platform edge and you grab it. Then: toward the stage or ↑ to climb, Jump to leap up, Attack to climb with a swing, away or ↓ to let go.

Gold Ink

Now and then a bottle of Gold Ink floats onto the stage. Grab it to get INKED: your fighter's own super move is ready (one use).

Cameos

A loose sketchbook page sometimes flutters in. Touch it and a side character from another Pencilbox game is sketched in to help you for a few seconds.

Gamepad

Stick or d-pad to move, A jump, X attack, B special, Y / RB super, Start pause. Menus: d-pad to move, A to choose, B to go back. With two players, a single gamepad is Player 2.

Touch

On-screen pad on the left, Jump / Attack / Special / Super on the right, ❚❚ to pause. Play in landscape.

Story Mode

Explore the prison with ←/→ and Jump, press ↑ at doors and cells. Win to free a soul, then talk — their mood decides which answer they'll trust. Enter continues, 1–3 answers, Esc skips a scene.
