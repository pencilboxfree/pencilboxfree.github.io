How To Play

Controls

← ↑ ↓ → — move (and aim, for seekers)
SPACE — fire the laser. Seekers only: a hider's space bar does nothing
Q / E — turn on the spot, or rotate a costume
SHIFT — sprint   C — change disguise in prep
T — chat   ESC — menu
Mouse aiming still works if you want it, and touch gets a stick and a fire button.

Hiders

Wear a costume that matches props already on the map, or paint yourself to match the floor. Stand still for a moment to lock in your blend. Move and you wobble — and wobbling props get shot. Survive to the final whistle and the hiders win.

Seekers

You start in the waiting room and cannot see or shoot anyone until the gate opens — there is a flap-and-dodge minigame in there. Once out, you cannot tag by touching. The laser is the only way. Watch for props that drift, wobble, or sit somewhere no one would put them. Wrong guesses cost you the cooldown — and the round timer.

Roles

Leave the role on Random and the game draws for you each round — and never gives you the same role three times in a row. You can also pick Hider or Seeker outright in the lobby.

Tells

A hider who has just moved leaves a faint dust puff and sways for a second. A costume standing alone with no twins nearby is a bad costume. Lasers stop at walls and tall props, so both sides can use cover.
