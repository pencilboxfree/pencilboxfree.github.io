HOW TO PLAY

  • WASD / Arrows — move  ·  Mouse — aim
  • Space or click — fire
  • Every gun has a cooldown between shots — watch the meter next to your ammo.
  • 1–6 — switch guns (or scroll the wheel, or click the grid). Swapping takes a moment, and each gun keeps its own magazine.
  • R — reload  ·  Shift — sprint  ·  Esc / P — pause
  • Use walls and crates for cover — lasers do not pass through them.
  • Enemies get tougher every wave. Survive as long as you can.
  • Coins are banked when the match ends — spend them in the Armory.
