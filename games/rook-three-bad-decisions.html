How To Play

Make Choices

Every scene ends with options. Click one — or press 1 2 3 4.

Each choice plays out a different scene. Many of them go badly. That is the point.

Button Mashing

Sometimes the game grabs the wheel:

  • SPACE — mash to power through
  • E — mash to force something open
  • X — mash to hold on
  • ← → — alternate arrows to keep balance

Beat the bar before the timer runs out. Fail and the story takes a worse turn.

Endings

Each job hides 16 main endings, 8 consequence endings and a few secret ones.

Consequence endings come from how you treat people — bribe, betray, steal, or be decent. They pay off much later, usually when you have forgotten about it.

Discovered endings are logged in the Ending Collection. The rest stay blurred out.

People Remember

When you see "He will remember that.", it is not decoration. That person comes back.

Help someone and they may help you at the worst moment. Cross them and they will pick a much better moment than you would.

Getting Around

After any ending you can retry the job, rewind to the last choice, or move on if the story allows.

Progress saves automatically in this browser. Esc abandons a run.

Press Enter or click to fast-forward dialogue.
